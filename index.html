CardWatch Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

CardWatch is a private seller-management application operated for 2 Buds Sports Cards. This policy explains how CardWatch collects, uses, stores, protects, and deletes information obtained through eBay.

Information CardWatch Accesses

After the eBay account owner grants permission, CardWatch may access authorized seller information such as listings, inventory, offers, orders, fulfillment information, selling analytics, advertising and campaign information, store information, account settings, and related eBay seller data permitted by the authorization scopes granted by the user.

How Information Is Used

CardWatch uses authorized eBay information solely to support seller operations, including monitoring listings, analyzing performance, preparing recommendations, tracking sales, reviewing advertising campaigns, maintaining historical performance records, and performing seller-approved eBay actions.

Data Sharing and Sale

CardWatch does not sell personal information. CardWatch does not intentionally disclose eBay account information to unrelated third parties except where required to operate the service, comply with law, protect the system, or satisfy applicable eBay developer requirements.

Data Storage and Security

CardWatch data is stored on systems controlled by 2 Buds Sports Cards. Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect stored information, authentication credentials, and authorization tokens from unauthorized access.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for CardWatch seller operations, recordkeeping, security, and applicable legal or platform requirements. CardWatch processes eBay marketplace account deletion notifications and removes or anonymizes applicable data in accordance with eBay developer requirements.

Authorization

eBay access is granted through eBay's authorization process. CardWatch does not receive or store the user's eBay password. Authorization may be revoked through eBay where supported.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as CardWatch features or applicable requirements change. The current version will remain available at this URL.
